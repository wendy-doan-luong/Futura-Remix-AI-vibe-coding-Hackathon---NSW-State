# 2nd prize Futura-Remix-AI-vibe-coding-Hackathon---NSW-State 2026
**Team**: Three and a half
**Track 1**: Future of Work and International Talent
## 📖 Overview

_**codecraft**_ is an AI-powered platform designed to turn AI skills into verified proof that employers trust. We bridge the gap between international graduate capabilities and Australian employer expectations by providing a secure, unbiased environment to complete real-world, role-specific tasks.
## ⚠️ The Problem

- Australia is a top destination for global talent, hosting hundreds of thousands of international students. However, a significant gap exists in the transition to the local workforce:

- High Demand, Low Supply: 97% of Australian hiring managers expect new hires to possess AI proficiency, yet 88% struggle to find talent with the right capabilities.

- Underemployment: More than 50% of international graduates in Australia are employed below their actual skill level.

- Primary Users: International students in CS, IT, data, and analytics often possess strong AI skills but lack the local experience, professional networks, and references needed to demonstrate their job readiness to Australian employers.
## 💡 The Solution

_**codecraft**_ helps candidates prove their capabilities.
Core Workflow

- Job Description to Actionable Items: Users paste a real job description (JD) into the platform.
- Dynamic Project Generation: The platform instantly generates a guided, 2-4 hour project specific to that role and company, producing a clear brief and a scoring rubric.
- In-Browser Workplace: Candidates work through the project using AI tools directly in the browser. We capture their reasoning and workflow.
- AI Literacy Assessment: An evaluation agent scores the submission against the mathematical rubric (ensuring consistent grading). The AI also reports a confidence figure so uncertainty remains visible.
- Mentor Verification: Because no high-stakes decisions should be fully automated, a human mentor reviews the score. The resulting verified evidence can then be shared directly with employers.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack (From my team members)
- Frontend: Next.js, Tailwind CSS
- Backend & Database: Supabase, PostgreSQL
- AI & Integration: OpenAI, Langfuse
- Search/Tools: DuckDuckGo

## 🎯 Impact
- For Students: Provides trusted proof of AI skills and helps improve them.
- For Employers: Verifies AI capability before the interview stage, reducing hiring risk.
- For Universities: Offers a measurable employability tool for improving graduate outcomes.
